# Expansion to 100+ guides: target list and wave tracker

Started 2026-10-20 at Praveen's direction ("develop at least 100+").
Current library before expansion: 14 (6 published + 8 draft).
Target: 95 new apps, for 109 total if all pan out. Apps that turn out to have
no real OAuth program get dropped (dead-end drafts stay in research/, like
Cricbuzz), so the final count may land slightly under 109 but above 100.

Rules (same as every previous wave): official docs only, read-only research,
provider-stated times only, everything else flagged unverified; guides enter
as status "draft" until spot-checked.
